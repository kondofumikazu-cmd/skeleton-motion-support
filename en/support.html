Getting started

  1. Open the app and allow camera access.
  2. Move back until your face, shoulders and both hands are in view.
  3. In good lighting, try opening your mouth, blinking and moving your arms and fingers.
  4. Use Record to start and stop, then save from the preview.

The default background is black. Even when the camera background is visible, recordings do not include camera footage.

If the camera does not work

On iPhone, check camera permission for Sign Motion in Settings. On Mac, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera. The Mac app’s Open camera permissions button takes you there.

If another app is using the camera, stop its capture. On Mac, select the connected camera again and choose Start camera.

If hand or face lines do not match

Avoid backlighting and keep your fingers visible and your hands apart. Tracking can be inaccurate or incomplete when body parts are hidden, outside the frame or moving quickly. iPhone and Mac use different tracking systems, so their outlines and accuracy differ.

On Mac, pupils are drawn as circles around estimated centers. The app is not a medical measurement tool and cannot diagnose gaze or eye movement.

About 60 fps

If the camera does not support 60 fps, capture uses an available rate such as 30 fps. Display updates, camera frames and new tracking results are separate rates; check them in the app’s status information. When processing falls behind during recording, the previous skeleton is reused.

Saving recordings

On iPhone, stop recording and choose Save to Photos or Share. Permission to add to Photos is requested when saving. On Mac, stop recording and choose Save as… to select a destination. Exported MP4 files play in apps such as QuickTime Player.

Recordings contain skeleton movement. Check with the person being recorded before sharing.

Requirements

iPhone: iOS 18 or later. Mac: macOS 14 or later and an available camera. The standalone native Mac app supports Apple silicon and Intel.

Camera tracking does not need an account, microphone or internet connection. Visiting these pages and sending email require a connection.
