Privacy policy

Applies to Sign Motion 1.0 (build 1, iOS and Mac)
Effective: October 4, 2026 / Last updated: October 5, 2026

Face, body and hand tracking runs on your device.

Face images and coordinates used for tracking are not sent to the developer’s or SDK providers’ servers. Videos you choose to record contain drawn facial outlines and movements.

2. Camera, face, body and hand data

2.1 Face data accessed and generated

After camera permission is granted, the app processes camera images containing faces on the device and estimates facial landmark coordinates for the face outline, nose, eyebrows, eyes, eyelids, iris area and lips. The iOS version processes normalized x and y coordinates and an estimated relative z coordinate; the Mac version processes face regions and facial feature coordinates. Blinking and mouth opening are displayed as changes in these positions. Body and hand positions are also estimated.

Compatible cameras may provide a depth map of the captured scene, which can include the facial area, and camera calibration information to assist motion tracking such as hand positioning. Face tracking also works without depth. Version 1.0 (build 1) does not use ARKit face tracking, facial authentication templates, face blendshape coefficients or facial transformation matrices.

2.2 Uses of face data

Face data is used only to display facial lines and movements on screen and to include that rendering in recordings started by the user. It is not used for authentication, identifying people, profiling, advertising, marketing, analytics, AI model training or sale. No other use of face data is planned.

2.3 SDKs, third parties and transmission

The iOS version runs the bundled MediaPipe Tasks Vision SDK locally; the Mac version runs Apple Vision locally. Face images and landmarks are processed within the user’s device. The app does not transmit them to Google, Apple, the developer or other third-party servers. The developer does not receive face data or provide, share or sell it to advertising platforms, analytics providers, data brokers or other third parties. User-directed saving and sharing of rendered recordings are described in Section 3.

3. Recordings, storage locations and user-directed sharing

Only when you start recording does the app create an MP4 of the skeleton on black. It includes drawn facial lines, blinks, mouth opening and body and finger movements, so it contains information derived from face data. It contains no camera footage or audio. Face images, numerical landmark arrays and depth maps are not saved as separate files or databases.

iOS recordings are stored in Documents/Recordings within the app’s private local container. Mac recordings are stored in Application Support/SkeletonMotion/Recordings within the app’s sandbox. There is no developer-operated server or app-operated cloud storage for face data or recordings.

On iOS, a copy goes to Photos or the selected sharing destination only when you choose Save to Photos or Share. On Mac, you select a destination with Save as… to export a copy. The app does not automatically transmit recordings.

Copies may leave the device through iCloud Photos, file synchronization, operating-system backups or sharing services according to your settings. Those services determine their storage locations, retention and deletion practices. The app does not implement its own automatic synchronization.

4. Permissions and withdrawing consent

  • Camera: Used to recognize face, body and hand movements locally. The app requests system permission before access. You can revoke permission in system camera settings.
  • Add to Photos (iOS): Requested when saving a recording to Photos. This does not request access to read existing photos.
  • Write to selected files (Mac): Used to export a recording to the destination you choose.

To stop camera use, put the iOS app in the background or choose Stop camera on Mac. Revoking permission does not delete existing recordings; see Section 5.

Version 1.0 (build 1) does not use the microphone, contacts, location or advertising identifiers. It has no advertising SDK, analytics SDK, developer-operated automatic crash reporting or account registration.

5. Face data retention and deletion

5.1 Images, depth and coordinates used for tracking

Face images, landmarks and depth data used for tracking are processed temporarily only in volatile memory on the device. Frames are replaced or released as processing advances. Latest results and SDK processing state may remain in memory while the app process runs, and are lost when that process terminates. They are not written to disk or retained as tracking history after restart. Stopping the camera stops new capture; in-flight processing may temporarily retain a frame until it finishes. Stopping does not mean that all memory is erased instantly.

5.2 Recordings containing facial renderings

Recordings have no automatic expiry. In-app recordings are retained until the user deletes them. In the iOS recording preview, Delete or Retake removes that recording file from the app. Close does not delete it. To remove all in-app recordings, including recordings whose previews were closed, use the system’s Delete App action to remove the app and its data. Offload App preserves data and does not remove all recordings.

On Mac, use Finder’s Go → Go to Folder to open ~/Library/Containers/jp.local.avatarmotion/Data/Library/Application Support/SkeletonMotion/Recordings and delete the in-app recordings. Quit the app before doing this; do not delete a recording while it is being written.

Delete Photos and exported copies separately in Photos or Finder. Follow the respective app’s instructions to permanently remove copies in Recently Deleted or Trash. Manage and delete backup and shared copies separately through those services. Deleting in-app recordings or uninstalling the app does not delete these copies.

The developer cannot access face data or recordings on your device and cannot delete them remotely. For deletion assistance, use the contact in Section 1 without attaching footage.

6. Support requests

If you email us, we use your email address, your name if provided and the content of your request only as needed to respond and investigate. We retain this information for as long as necessary to handle the request, and delete it when no longer needed. You may request deletion using the contact in Section 1.

You do not need to attach face photos, camera footage or skeleton videos. We do not request face data as support material.

This information site is hosted on GitHub Pages. The developer has not added advertising or analytics scripts. GitHub processes and logs information such as IP addresses for security when you visit. This website has no feature for uploading face images, facial landmarks or recordings.

See the GitHub General Privacy Statement for details.
