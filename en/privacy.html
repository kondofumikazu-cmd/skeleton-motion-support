Privacy policy

Applies to Sign Motion (iPhone and Mac)
Effective and last updated: October 4, 2026

Face, body and hand tracking runs on your device.

The app has no feature that sends camera footage or tracking results to the developer’s servers or to advertising or analytics providers.

1. Developer and contact

Developer: KONDO FUMIKAZU
kondofumikazu@icloud.com

Please use this address for questions about the handling of personal information.

2. Camera, face, body and hand data

The app estimates the positions of the face outline, eyebrows, eyes, mouth, body and hands from camera images to display a skeleton and create videos. Supported iPhone cameras may provide depth data to assist motion tracking.

The iPhone version uses MediaPipe and the Mac version uses Apple Vision. Processing runs on the device. Images, depth data and coordinates used for tracking are held temporarily in memory and discarded as processing advances or stops. The app does not record or save raw camera footage or audio.

Face and motion data are not used for identity verification, facial authentication, advertising, identification or profiling. The developer does not collect, sell or provide this data to third parties.

3. Recording and saving

A video of the skeleton on black is saved locally only when you start recording. Videos include face outlines and body and finger movements, but no camera footage or audio.

On iPhone, you can choose to add a recording to Photos or share it. On Mac, you choose a destination when exporting. The app does not automatically send videos elsewhere.

If you use photo or file synchronization, operating-system backups or sharing services, their settings and policies apply. The app does not automatically synchronize recordings between iPhone and Mac.

4. Permissions and choices

  • Camera: Required for motion tracking. You can revoke access in system settings.
  • Add to Photos (iPhone): Requested when saving a recording to Photos. This does not request access to read existing photos.
  • Write to selected files (Mac): Used to export a recording to the destination you choose.

The app does not use the microphone, contacts, location or advertising identifiers. It has no advertising SDK, analytics SDK, developer-operated automatic crash reporting or account registration.

5. Retention and deletion

Recordings may remain on the device until you delete them. For videos available in the iPhone recording preview, use Delete to remove them from the app. Delete exported copies separately in Photos or Finder. Manage backup and shared copies through the respective services.

The developer cannot access recordings on your device. If you need help finding or deleting a recording, contact us without attaching footage.

6. Support requests

If you email us, we use your email address, your name if provided and the content of your request only as needed to respond and investigate. We retain this information for as long as necessary to handle the request, and delete it when no longer needed. You may request deletion using the contact address above.

Please avoid attaching face photos, camera footage, skeleton videos or other personal information unnecessarily. If material is needed to investigate an issue, we will explain what is needed separately.

7. This website

This information site is hosted on GitHub Pages. The developer has not added advertising or analytics scripts. GitHub processes and logs information such as IP addresses for security when you visit. This website traffic is separate from the app’s camera tracking.

See the GitHub General Privacy Statement for details.

8. Changes to this policy

If features or data handling change, this page will be updated with a revised date.
