Privacy policy

Applies to the iPhone and iPad version of Sign Motion with 2D skeleton display and optional audio recording
Effective: October 4, 2026 / Last updated: October 7, 2026

Face, body and hand tracking runs on your device.

Face images and coordinates used for tracking are not sent to the developer’s or SDK providers’ servers. Videos you choose to record contain drawn facial outlines and movements.

2. Camera, face, body and hand data

2.1 Face data accessed and generated

After camera permission is granted, the app processes camera images containing faces on the device and estimates facial landmark coordinates for the face outline, nose, eyebrows, eyes, eyelids, iris area and lips. It processes normalized x and y coordinates and an estimated relative z coordinate. It also estimates facial expression coefficients for cheek puff, smiles, blinking and mouth movements on the device, using them for 2D rendering. Blinking and mouth opening are displayed as changes in these positions. Body and hand positions are also estimated. Eyebrow, gaze, lip, jaw and nose expression coefficients are used, and separate cheek shape is estimated against a neutral front-facing baseline. Teeth and tongue display uses transient color samples from a limited inner-mouth region. It may confuse tongue with gums and is not precise tongue tracking.

Compatible cameras may provide a depth map of the captured scene, which can include the facial area, and camera calibration information to assist motion tracking such as hand positioning. Face tracking also works without depth. The app does not use ARKit face tracking, facial authentication templates, facial transformation matrices or 3D models.

2.2 Uses of face data

Face data is used only to display facial lines and movements on screen and to include that rendering in recordings started by the user. It is not used for authentication, identifying people, profiling, advertising, marketing, analytics, AI model training or sale. No other use of face data is planned.

2.3 SDKs, third parties and transmission

The app runs the bundled MediaPipe Tasks Vision SDK locally. Face images and landmarks are processed within the user’s device. The app does not transmit them to Google, Apple, the developer or other third-party servers. The developer does not receive face data or provide, share or sell it to advertising platforms, analytics providers, data brokers or other third parties. User-directed saving and sharing of rendered recordings are described in Section 3.

3. Recordings, storage locations and user-directed sharing

Only when you start recording does the app create an MP4 of the skeleton on black. It includes drawn facial lines, blinks, mouth opening and body and finger movements, so it contains information derived from face data. It contains no camera footage. Audio recording is off by default. If you enable it in Settings and start recording, microphone audio is included. Face images, numerical landmark arrays and depth maps are not saved as separate files or databases.

iOS recordings are stored in Documents/Recordings within the app’s private local container. There is no developer-operated server or app-operated cloud storage for face data or recordings.

On iOS, a copy goes to Photos or the selected sharing destination only when you choose Save to Photos or Share. The app does not automatically transmit recordings.

Copies may leave the device through iCloud Photos, file synchronization, operating-system backups or sharing services according to your settings. Those services determine their storage locations, retention and deletion practices. The app does not implement its own automatic synchronization.

4. Permissions and withdrawing consent

  • Camera: Used to recognize face, body and hand movements locally. The app requests system permission before access. You can revoke permission in system camera settings.
  • Add to Photos (iOS): Requested when saving a recording to Photos. This does not request access to read existing photos.
  • Microphone: Permission is requested when starting a recording with audio enabled. Microphone audio is used in that recording. You can turn audio recording off in Settings.

To stop camera use, put the app in the background. Revoking permission does not delete existing recordings; see Section 5.

The app does not use contacts, location or advertising identifiers. It has no advertising SDK, analytics SDK, developer-operated automatic crash reporting or account registration.

5. Face data retention and deletion

5.1 Images, depth and coordinates used for tracking

Face images, landmarks, expression coefficients, oral color samples and depth data used for tracking are processed temporarily only in volatile memory on the device. Frames are replaced or released as processing advances. Latest results and SDK processing state may remain in memory while the app process runs, and are lost when that process terminates. Release builds do not write them to disk or retain them as tracking history after restart. Debug builds used for development and verification store diagnostic logs on the device, including detection counts, processing rates and some expression coefficients. These logs contain no camera images or full facial landmark arrays, and the app does not automatically transmit them. Stopping the camera stops new capture; in-flight processing may temporarily retain a frame until it finishes. Stopping does not mean that all memory is erased instantly.

5.2 Recordings containing facial renderings

Recordings have no automatic expiry. In-app recordings are retained until the user deletes them. In the iOS recording preview, Delete or Retake removes that recording file from the app. Close does not delete it. To remove all in-app recordings, including recordings whose previews were closed, use the system’s Delete App action to remove the app and its data. Offload App preserves data and does not remove all recordings.

Delete Photos and exported copies separately in Photos or Finder. Follow the respective app’s instructions to permanently remove copies in Recently Deleted or Trash. Manage and delete backup and shared copies separately through those services. Deleting in-app recordings or uninstalling the app does not delete these copies.

The developer cannot access face data or recordings on your device and cannot delete them remotely. For deletion assistance, use the contact in Section 1 without attaching footage.

6. Support requests

If you email us, we use your email address, your name if provided and the content of your request only as needed to respond and investigate. We retain this information for as long as necessary to handle the request, and delete it when no longer needed. You may request deletion using the contact in Section 1.

You do not need to attach face photos, camera footage or skeleton videos. We do not request face data as support material.
